# TS-ICL (Time-Indexed In-Context Learning)

## Overview
A probabilistic In-Context Learning (ICL) foundation model built on an Encoder–Regressor Transformer framework that unifies time-series forecasting, missing-value imputation, and irregular sampling under timestamp-aligned regression with synthetic DAG causal pretraining.

## Literature Usage
- [[2026_TS_ICL_Time_Indexed_Foundation_Model]] — Introduces TS-ICL, achieving SOTA zero-shot imputation on fm-impute-bench and competitive forecasting on fev-bench with native covariate support.
