He had half a year of research experience with the Institute for Automo- bile Research, Polytechnic University of Madrid, Spain, in 2018. His research interests include intel- ligent transportation and time series forecasting applications using cutting-edge deep learning and reinforcement learning techniques. Di Wu (Member, IEEE) received the M.Sc. degree from Peking University, Beijing, China, in 2013, and the Ph.D. degree from McGill University, Montreal, Canada, in 2018. He is currently a Senior Staff Research Scientist with the Samsung AI Center- Montreal and an Adjunct Professor with McGill University. Before joining Samsung, he was a Post-Doctoral Researcher with Montreal MILA and Stanford University. His research interests include designing algorithms, such as reinforcement learn- ing and operation research, for sequential decision- making problems, and data-efficient machine learning algorithms, such as transfer learning, meta-learning, and multitask learning. He is also interested in leveraging such algorithms for applications in real-world systems, such as communications systems, smart grids, and intelligent transportation systems. Benoit Boulet (Senior Member, IEEE) received the bachelor's degree in applied sciences from Univer- sité Laval in 1990, the M.E. degree in electrical engineering from McGill University in 1992, and the Ph.D. degree in electrical engineering from the Uni- versity of Toronto in 1996. In 1998, he joined McGill University, where he is currently a Professor with the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, and the Director of the Technological Innovation and Entrepreneurship Centre, McGill Engine. He is also an Associate Vice-Principal of McGill Innovation and Partnerships. From 2014 to 2020, he was the Associate Dean (Research and Innovation) with the Faculty of Engineering. He is the former Director and a current member of the McGill Centre for Intelligent Machines, where he heads the Intelligent Automation Laboratory. His research interests include the design and data-driven control of electric vehicles and renewable energy systems, machine learning applied to biomedical systems, and robust industrial control.
