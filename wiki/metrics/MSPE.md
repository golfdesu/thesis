# MSPE

Mean Squared Percentage Error; percentage-based analogue of MSE, related to [[MAPE]].

## Referenced in this knowledge base

*(none recorded)*

MSPE shares weaknesses with [[MAPE]]: undefined for zero actuals and asymmetric between over- and under-forecasts. Compare with [[MSE]] for scale-sensitive evaluation and [[NRMSE]] for normalized comparisons.
