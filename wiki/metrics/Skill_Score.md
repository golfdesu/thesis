# Skill_Score

## Overview
Skill score — percentage improvement over Seasonal Naive baseline; S = 100*(1-G) where G is geometric mean relative error (Shchur et al. 2025).

## Literature Usage
- [[2025_Ansari_Chronos_2_Univariate_to_Universal]] — **Skill score** vs Seasonal Naive reported on all 3 benchmarks (Tables 3-5); e.g. fev-bench SQL 47.3% (Chronos-2), GIFT-Eval WQL 51.4%, Chronos Bench II WQL 46.6%.
