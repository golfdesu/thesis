# Depth of Discharge

Fraction of battery capacity cycled between charges; degradation-related battery feature.

## Referenced in this knowledge base

[[2021_Dabbaghjamanesh_RL_Q_Learning_EV_Load]]

## Usable Window
Manufacturers typically constrain the usable DoD window (e.g. 10-90%) to limit degradation; charging-demand models that respect this window produce more realistic session-energy targets than those assuming the full nameplate capacity.
