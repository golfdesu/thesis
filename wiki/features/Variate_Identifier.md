# Variate Identifier

## Overview
Token-level identifier distinguishing different variates in flattened multivariate time series, enabling permutation equivariance and invariance.

## Literature Usage
- [[2024_Unified_Training_Universal_Time_Series_Transformers]] — Any-variate Attention uses binary variate bias scalars ($u^1$ for intra-variate, $u^2$ for inter-variate) to distinguish channels without imposing fixed channel ordering.
