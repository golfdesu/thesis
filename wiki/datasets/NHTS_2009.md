# NHTS 2009 (US National Household Travel Survey)

## 📊 Overview
The 2009 US **National Household Travel Survey** — the standard national source of individual travel behavior (trip times, distances, vehicle usage) used to derive plug-in probability profiles, daily mileage distributions, and PHEV charging-event statistics for EV demand modeling. Frequently paired with UC Davis PH&EV Center data for stochastic charging-demand models.

## 🔗 Access Links
- **ORNL NHTS portal**: https://nhts.ornl.gov
