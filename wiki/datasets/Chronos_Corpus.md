# Chronos Corpus

## Overview
Pretraining corpus from Ansari et al. 2024 (Chronos) — select datasets (Table 6 of [[2025_Ansari_Chronos_2_Univariate_to_Universal]]) reused in Chronos-2 real-univariate mix along with GIFT-Eval pretraining corpus and synthetic generators.

## Literature Usage
- [[2025_Liu_Sundial_Highly_Capable_Time_Series_Foundation_Models]] — 94B-point subset of TimeBench (9.11%) — see TimeBench composition.
- [[2025_Ansari_Chronos_2_Univariate_to_Universal]] — Real univariate component of Chronos-2 pretraining (Table 6 lists Electricity, M4, Solar, Traffic, Wiki, Buildings 900K, etc. with frequencies and series counts).

## Data Availability
- Ansari et al. 2024; HuggingFace.
