# LargeST

## Overview
Large-scale traffic benchmark dataset from the California Department of Transportation Performance Measurement System (PeMS) curated by Liu et al. (NeurIPS 2023). Comprises 42,333 traffic sensors with 4,452,510,528 observations at 5-minute resolution across California highways.
