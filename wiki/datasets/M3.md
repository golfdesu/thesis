The **M3-Competition** dataset (Makridakis & Hibon, *International Journal of Forecasting* 2000): **3,003 series** across yearly (645), quarterly (756), monthly (1,394) and other (208) categories drawn from demographic, finance, economics and industry domains.

Standard protocol: forecasts over horizons matched to frequency (18 yearly, 8 quarterly, 18 monthly) evaluated with sMAPE/MASE; the "other" category contains the famous sunspot/mortality series. Descendant of the M-competitions lineage that led to [[M4]] and [[M5_Dataset]]; OWA-style metrics (cf. [[MASE]], [[OWA]]) originate here.
