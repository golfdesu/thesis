# BuildingsBench

## Overview
Large-scale benchmark of building energy consumption datasets curated by NREL (Emami et al., NeurIPS 2023 Datasets & Benchmarks). Contains real and simulated building load profiles (Buildings-900K, BDG-2 Panther/Fox/Rat/Bear, Low Carbon London, SMART, IDEAL, Sceaux, Borealis) totaling over 15.7B observations.
