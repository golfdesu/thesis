# SubseasonalClimateUSA

## Overview
Subseasonal climate forecasting benchmark dataset in the USA (Mouatadid et al., 2023) covering precipitation (1948-1978) and temperature/precipitation (1979-2023) across 862 grid points at daily resolution (23.8M obs).
