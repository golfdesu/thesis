# Fujian Province EV Charging Station Data

## 📊 Overview
EV charging data from **one charging station in Fujian Province, China**, covering **January – April 2022** at a **15-min sampling resolution** (96 time steps defined as one week at this granularity). Funded by State Grid Fujian Electric Power Co., Ltd.; used for VMD-decomposition-based hybrid forecasting where intrinsic mode functions feed Prophet and LSTM components.
