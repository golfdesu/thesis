# Favorita Retail

Favorita Retail — the **Corporación Favorita Grocery Sales Forecasting** dataset (Kaggle competition, Ecuadorian grocery chain). Verified 2026-08: daily unit sales per store-item with promotions, holidays/oil-price covariates; training data spans **2013-01-01 to 2017-08-15**, 54 stores. Used by TFT (Lim et al., 2021) as a real-world retail-demand benchmark.
