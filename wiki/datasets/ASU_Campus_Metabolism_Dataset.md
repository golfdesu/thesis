# ASU Campus Metabolism Dataset

ASU Campus Metabolism — ASU's Energy Information System dashboard ("Campus Metabolism", live at cm.asu.edu since 2008) metering electricity, chilled water, heating and solar across Tempe campus buildings. Verified 2026-08: public research release (github.com/hepei002/ASU_Data) contains **daily** load of the integrated campus energy system **2018–2023** (electrical kW, cooling tons/h, heating mmBTU/h); a Scientific Data companion (HEEW) built on meters from 147 individual buildings + 4 aggregated communities. Note: the open release is daily-resolution — check any paper claiming hourly/15-min use against its own preprocessing.
