# KDD Cup 2022 (SDWPF)

## Overview
Spatial Dynamic Wind Power Forecasting dataset (Zhou et al., NeurIPS 2022) with 134 wind turbines, 10-minute interval, active power target and 9 dynamic covariates (4.7M obs).
