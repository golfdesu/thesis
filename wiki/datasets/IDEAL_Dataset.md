# IDEAL Dataset

The **IDEAL Household Energy Dataset** — EPSRC-funded *Intelligent Domestic Energy Advice Loop* project, University of Edinburgh. Verified 2026-08 (Pullinger et al., *Scientific Data* 8, 2021, doi:10.1038/s41597-021-00921-y): 255 UK homes in Edinburgh/Lothians/south Fife, main study phase Aug 2016 – Jun 2018 (~23 months). Meter-level electricity apparent power at **1 Hz**, gas per fixed-volume pulse, 12-second room temperature/humidity/light and boiler pipe temperatures; individual appliance-level data for 39 homes; anonymised survey metadata. Released via Edinburgh DataShare (DOI 10.7488/ds/2836).
