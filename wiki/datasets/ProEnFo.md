# ProEnFo

## Overview
Open-source benchmark for probabilistic energy forecasting (Wang et al., 2023), providing load datasets with weather covariates (GEF12, GEF14, GEF17, PDB, Spanish, BDG-2 Hog/Bull/Cockatoo, ELF).
