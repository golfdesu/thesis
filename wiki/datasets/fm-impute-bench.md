# fm-impute-bench

## Overview
A standardized zero-shot time series imputation benchmark comprising 132 univariate tasks and 24 known-covariate tasks across energy, climate, healthcare, and economic domains.

## Literature Usage
- [[2026_TS_ICL_Time_Indexed_Foundation_Model]] — Primary zero-shot imputation evaluation testbed where TS-ICL establishes a new state-of-the-art across NMAE and CRPS metrics.
