The **Tourism forecasting competition** dataset (Athanasopoulos, Hyndman, Song & Wu 2011): **366 series** of visitor flows (monthly/quarterly/yearly) from multiple countries and regions.

Protocol: horizon-matched forecasts (24 months, 8 quarters, 4 years) evaluated with MASE-like relative measures; famous for showing seasonal-naive and exponential-smoothing baselines beating sophisticated models — a cautionary result echoed in EV forecasting baseline studies (cf. [[Persistence_Model]], [[Historical_Average]]).
