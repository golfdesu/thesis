# 📁 CrowdCharge / UK Power Networks EV Trial Dataset

## 🔗 Access Links & Repositories
- **CrowdCharge Official Site**: [CrowdCharge EV Network](https://www.crowdcharge.com/)
- **UK Power Networks Open Data**: [UK Power Networks EV Trial Data](https://myelectricavenue.info/)

## Overview
Real-world EV charging dataset collected during UK EV trial projects (CrowdCharge / My Electric Avenue / UK Power Networks) evaluating day-ahead market load curves.

## Key Papers Utilizing Dataset
- `[[2024_EV_Load_Forecasting_DAM]]` - Bampos et al. (2024): Evaluated Day-Ahead Market (DAM) forecasting on CrowdCharge, Palo Alto, and ACN datasets.
