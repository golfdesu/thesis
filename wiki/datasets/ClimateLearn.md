# ClimateLearn

## Overview
Machine learning library and dataset benchmark for weather and climate modeling (Nguyen et al., NeurIPS 2023 Workshop), providing ERA5 reanalysis and CMIP6 climate model projections at 2.8125-degree grid resolution (64x128 grid, 4.12B observations total).
