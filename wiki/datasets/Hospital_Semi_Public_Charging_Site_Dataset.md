# Hospital Semi Public Charging Site Dataset

Use-case dataset of **Van Kriekinge et al. (2021)**, *Day-Ahead Forecast of Electric Vehicle Charging Demand with Deep Neural Networks* (WEVJ 12(4):178) — charging sessions recorded at a **hospital semi-public site in Brussels, Belgium**: six chargers with two 22 kW Type 2 connectors each, sessions **mid-June 2018 to end-July 2019**, 95.2% of sessions from recurring commuters. Day-ahead demand forecast at **15-min resolution** (96 steps/day); LSTM variants with calendar/weather features cut MAE/RMSE by ~29%/19% vs the literature baseline (MAE < 1 kW). Data not publicly released.
